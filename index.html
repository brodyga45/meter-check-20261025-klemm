What columns and records does this need?

Equipment ID, equipment type, previous reading, current reading and reading at last service. A unit column is optional; if supplied it must agree with the type rule. Accepted unit names include SMR/hours and ODOMETER/km. A batch must contain one row per equipment ID. Readings support 0 through 1,000,000,000.999. Negative values, exponent notation and comma decimals are rejected rather than guessed. Empty rows inside a table, duplicate/blank headers and inconsistent row widths must be corrected.

Intervals and warnings are in meter units, not calendar dates. One interval and one next service per equipment type are supported. Multiple concurrent service schedules, automatic meter replacement reconciliation and live Google Sheets updates are outside this checker. To switch from the example, replace the readings and type rules, then confirm them.
